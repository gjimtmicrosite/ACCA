GJIMT ACCA Training Program

Duration: 24–28 Months | For Class 12 (Senior Secondary) Students

Level 1: Knowledge

  • Business & Technology (BT)
  • Financial Accounting (FA)
  • Management Accounting (MA)

Level 2: Applied Skills

  • Corporate & Business Law (LW)
  • Taxation (TX)
  • Financial Reporting (FR)
  • Performance Management (PM)
  • Financial Management (FM)
  • Audit & Assurance (AA)

Level 3: Strategic Professional

  • Essentials: SBR, SBL
  • Options: AFM, AAA, ATX, APM
  • AFM + AAA (Recommended)

Academic Calendar Logic

  • 2 papers per ACCA sitting
  • 12–14 weeks teaching + 2 weeks revision
  • Weekday classes + weekend practice
  • Total: 7 exam cycles

Month-wise Plan

Phase 1 (Months 1–3)

BT + MA

  • Month 1: Business structure, costing basics
  • Month 2: HR, budgeting
  • Month 3: Revision + September Exam

Phase 2 (Months 4–6)

  • Financial Accounting
  • December Exam

Phase 3 (Months 7–9)

  • PM + TX
  • March Exam

Phase 4 (Months 10–12)

  • FR + AA
  • June Exam

Phase 5 (Months 13–15)

  • FM + LW
  • September Exam

Phase 6 (Months 16–18)

  • SBL + SBR
  • December Exam

Phase 7 (Months 19–21)

  • AFM + AAA
  • March Exam

Weekly Teaching Timetable

Day Hours Activity
Mon–Fri 3 hrs/day Theory + Problems
Saturday 4 hrs Case Studies + Past Papers
Sunday - Self Study

Assessment Plan

  • Weekly: Quizzes
  • Monthly: Section Tests
  • Pre-Exam: 3–4 Mock Tests
