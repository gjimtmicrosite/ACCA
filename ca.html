CA Syllabus & Structure

The CA syllabus consists of 16 papers divided into 3 levels:
CA Foundation Level - 4 Papers
CA Intermediate Level - 6 Papers
CA Final Level - 6 Papers

Passing Score

Minimum 40% in each subject and 50% aggregate required to pass.

Exam Schedule

Foundation & Final: June & December
Intermediate: May & November

CA Foundation Level

  • Accounting
  • Business Laws
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • Business Economics

CA Intermediate Level

  • Advanced Accounting
  • Corporate Laws
  • Taxation
  • Cost & Management Accounting
  • Auditing & Ethics
  • Financial Management + Strategic Management

CA Final Level

  • Financial Reporting
  • Advanced Financial Management
  • Advanced Auditing,Assurance & Professional Ethics
  • Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation
  • Indirect Tax Laws
  • Integrated Business Solutions
