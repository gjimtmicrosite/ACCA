GJIMT – ACCA Professional Training Program

For B.Com Graduates (5 ACCA Exemptions)

Duration: 16 Months | 8 Papers | Hybrid Mode

About the Program

GJIMT offers a structured 16-month ACCA preparation pathway enabling B.Com graduates to complete the remaining 8 ACCA papers with expert faculty, exam-focused coaching, and strong placement support. The program is aligned with ACCA quarterly exam sittings to ensure maximum pass rates.

Papers Covered (After 5 Exemptions)

Applied Skills

  • Performance Management (PM)
  • Financial Reporting (FR)
  • Audit & Assurance (AA)
  • Financial Management (FM)

Strategic Professional

  • Strategic Business Leader (SBL)
  • Strategic Business Reporting (SBR)
  • Advanced Financial Management (AFM)
  • Advanced Audit & Assurance (AAA)

16-Month Training Plan

  • Phase 1 (Months 1–4): PM + FM → June Attempt
  • Phase 2 (Months 5–8): AA + FR → September Attempt
  • Phase 3 (Months 9–12): SBL + SBR → December Attempt
  • Phase 4 (Months 13–16): AFM + AAA → March Attempt

Weekly Teaching Schedule

  • Monday–Friday: 3 hours/day – Concept teaching & problem solving
  • Saturday: 4 hours – Case studies & past papers
  • Sunday: Self-study & recorded lectures
  • Total: 18–20 contact hours per week

Teaching Methodology

  • Exam-oriented approach
  • Weekly quizzes & assignments
  • Monthly sectional tests
  • 3–4 full-length mock exams before each sitting
