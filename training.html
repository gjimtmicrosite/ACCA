Program Overview

For: BCom Graduates

Total Duration: 16 Months (4 Exam Cycles)

Papers Covered (8)

  • Applied Skills (4): PM, FR, AA, FM
  • Strategic Professional (4): SBL, SBR, AFM*, AAA*

*AFM + AAA selected as most employable option combination

Academic Calendar Logic

  • 2 papers per ACCA sitting
  • Each block: 12–14 teaching weeks + 2 revision weeks
  • Classes: 5 days/week + weekend practice
  • Total duration: 4 exam cycles (16 months)

Phase 1 (Months 1–4): PM + FM

Month 1
  • PM: Costing techniques, variances, budgeting
  • FM: Time value of money, investment appraisal
  • Weekly numericals
Month 2
  • PM: Decision making, performance measurement
  • FM: Working capital, cost of capital
  • Section tests
Month 3
  • PM: Advanced topics + case questions
  • FM: Risk management, business valuation
  • 2 mocks per paper
Month 4 (Revision + Exam)
  • Full syllabus revision
  • 3 full mocks each
  • June Sitting (PM + FM)

Phase 2 (Months 5–8): AA + FR

Month 5
  • AA: Audit framework, ethics, risk
  • FR: Conceptual framework, IFRS basics
Month 6
  • AA: Internal control, audit evidence
  • FR: Financial statements preparation
Month 7
  • AA: Audit reports, completion
  • FR: Consolidation & cash flow
  • Weekly case writing
Month 8 (Revision + Exam)
  • Intensive problem practice
  • 3 mocks each
  • September Sitting (AA + FR)

Phase 3 (Months 9–12): SBL + SBR

Month 9
  • SBL: Strategy models, governance, leadership
  • SBR: Advanced IFRS standards
Month 10
  • SBL: Integrated case analysis
  • SBR: Groups & complex reporting
Month 11
  • SBL: Report writing, professional skills
  • SBR: Interpretation & ethics
  • Daily case practice
Month 12 (Revision + Exam)
  • 4-hour mock simulations
  • Answer structuring drills
  • December Sitting (SBL + SBR)

Phase 4 (Months 13–16): AFM + AAA

Month 13
  • AFM: Investment appraisal, risk hedging
  • AAA: Audit planning, risk assessment
Month 14
  • AFM: Business valuation, M&A
  • AAA: Evidence & procedures
Month 15
  • AFM: International finance
  • AAA: Reporting & completion
  • Full case studies
Month 16 (Revision + Exam)
  • Scenario practice only
  • 3 mocks each
  • March Sitting (AFM + AAA)
