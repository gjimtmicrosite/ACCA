CPA Overview

  • CPA is a 4-hour exam divided into four sections.
  • Complete all sections within 18 months.
  • Sections can be taken in any order.
  • Exam windows: Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec.

Paper 1: Financial Accounting & Reporting

  • Conceptual Framework, Standard Setting and Financial Reporting
  • Select Financial Statement Accounts
  • Select Transactions

Paper 2: Auditing & Attestation

  • Ethics, Professional Responsibilities & General Principles
  • Assessing Risk and Developing a Planned Response
  • Performing Further Procedures & Obtaining Evidence
  • Forming Conclusions & Reporting

Paper 3: Regulation

  • Ethics, Professional Responsibilities & Federal Tax Procedures
  • Business LawFederal Taxation of Property Transactions
  • Federal Taxation of Individuals
  • Federal Taxation of Entities

Paper 4: Business Environment & Concepts

  • Corporate Governance
  • Economic Analysis
  • Financial Management
  • IT & Operations
